PD Gait Speed MCID Reference  (Hass et al., JNPT 2014)

Tier Threshold Clinical interpretation This cohort (n=44)
0 — No improvement
 < 0.06 m/s Change below the small MCID; unlikely to be perceived as meaningful 23 (52.3%)
1 — Small
 0.06 to <0.14 m/s Perceptible, small improvement; may affect some daily walking tasks 8 (18.2%)
2 — Moderate
 0.14 to <0.22 m/s Moderate improvement; likely to translate to functional walking gains 5 (11.4%)
3 — Large
 ≥ 0.22 m/s Substantial improvement; corresponds to large effect size in PD motor outcomes 8 (18.2%)

Thresholds derived from distribution-based analyses and effect size metrics for persons with PD on medication: Hass CJ et al. J Neurol Phys Ther. 2014;38(4):233–238. doi:10.1097/NPT.0000000000000049.
A general-pathology MCID of 0.10–0.17 m/s has also been reported across mixed populations: Bohannon & Glenney. J Eval Clin Pract. 2014;20(4):295–300.

⚠ Research use only — exploratory prototype, not validated for individual clinical decisions. Based on a preliminary single-center study (n = 44, NCKUH, Taiwan) without external validation. The LOOCV RMSE (0.137 m/s) exceeds the inter-tier interval (0.08 m/s); tier assignments carry substantial uncertainty at the individual-patient level. Results should not replace clinical judgment.
